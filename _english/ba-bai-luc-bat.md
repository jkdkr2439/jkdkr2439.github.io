## The Last Picture House

The old cinema hangs closed and bare.  
The projectionist remains inside the dark.  
The reel has mildewed into silence.  
Figures from another year cross a screen gone white.  
Beyond the porch, grass rises green.  
He holds out his hand and catches the crumpled dust of night.  
The dying lamp sings the shadows down.  
Only the empty seats remain, their soft dust waiting for whom?

## Seed Through the Flood

Water climbs over the village granary roof.  
She clasps the seed and makes her way across the bridge.  
The thatch goes under; the mulberry fields disappear.  
Above the drifting water, she carries the sleeping grain.  
By morning the flood has slipped below the hill.  
She presses seed into the brown alluvial earth.  
Green shoots rise beside the mulberry tips.  
No one remembers who carried grief through the rain.

## The Cloud Scribe

High on the hill stands an aging tower.  
The keeper writes down clouds as monsoon days pass.  
The silent instrument tastes the sour rain.  
Below, the streets have entered the season of wind and water.  
One night, blank paper; a sparse window.  
Dry ink crawls out of sight, as though the hour had not yet passed.  
Dawn falls across the clouded page.  
No one reads the record waiting at its end.
