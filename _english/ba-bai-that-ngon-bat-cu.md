## The Ancient Citadel

The lonely citadel listens into emptiness.  
Night rain at the spent watch covers the river wharf.  
A warhorse returns through the gate, bearing no master.  
An orphan drinks alone beneath the eastern eaves.  
Wind and cloud have altered; the human world runs out.  
The affairs of floating life mislay their bronze seal.  
One failing lamp illuminates the old chronicle.  
A thousand wordless autumns bury the city eastward.

## The Star Terrace

The lonely star terrace looks into celestial frost.  
At the final watch, an old man faces the far horizon.  
Returning moonlight strikes the ancient mirror.  
Ink-marks of constellations enter the lengthening night.  
The human calendar forgets the shapes of heaven.  
A new meteorology assigns the old land its weather.  
One point of cold light shines upon the world's affairs.  
The speechless old man passes into impermanence.

## The Keeper of Seed

The common people meet disaster and gather secret grief.  
An old woman bears her burden across the broken bridge.  
Ten thousand seeds lie stored for those who come after.  
One body breasts the flood to guard the mulberry fields.  
The annals of calamity preserve no trace of her name.  
A generation rises again and calls itself the beginning.  
One tender shoot breaks open the buried vein of earth.  
No one remembers the one who carried the first work.
