## 1. Nameless in the Register

At sunset the border gate locks the boundless road.  
The old traveller comes home to find his fields erased.  
Only the dog knows its former master by the empty landing.  
In a single night, the homeland has become another sky.

## 2. The Night Courier's Horse

Through the long night, hooves drive on the dying watch.  
A bodiless command urges them beyond the city.  
The rider dreams the loosened rein will free the four horizons.  
One fallen rating, and no road leads to a city.

## 3. The City's Eyes

At night, ten thousand city eyes burn through layered glass.  
With every human step, a shadow drops against the wall.  
Behind a bolted door, a person wonders what the charge might be.  
No watch is called; moon and wind turn to frost inside the gaze.

## 4. Taxing the Shade

The avenue is opened and the trees are stripped of shadow.  
Ten thousand households bid for one small piece of ease.  
Under the noon sun, the poor choke on the naked road.  
Beneath rented cloud, the wealthy laugh in leisure.  
Bronze pillars rise and partition all beneath heaven.  
Impartial daylight is converted into merchandise.  
A little child still searches for the shadow of a leaf.  
His mother says: that shade was never theirs to grant.

## 5. The Clerk at the Final Watch

At the spent watch, the clerk records each name.  
A hundred lives enter the book; his own alone is gone.  
The vermilion brush wears down his fingers and his shade.  
Black ink fills the files with an indifferent void.  
The day withdraws; moss gathers on the office seal.  
He searches a thousand pages and cannot find himself.  
He turns for home, and the wind knows who has passed.  
Only the village register declares him nameless.
