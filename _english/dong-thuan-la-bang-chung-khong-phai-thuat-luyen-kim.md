Thirty compasses point north. This is impressive if the compasses were
independently made, checked, and placed away from interference. It is less
impressive if all thirty are painted needles glued to one table.

Agreement is visible. The structure that produced it is not.

> Consensus can be strong evidence when informed investigators converge
> through sufficiently independent and self-correcting routes. Agreement does
> not turn a proposition true by social force.

## Why convergence matters

Different investigators carry different errors. When they use relevant
methods, expose claims to criticism, and still converge, many local mistakes
are filtered out. Agreement then provides information even to readers unable
to reproduce the underlying work.

This is not voting reality into existence. It is using the pattern of
successful inquiry as evidence about which claim has survived.

## Independence comes in degrees

No research community is perfectly independent. Members share instruments,
education, journals, datasets, and concepts. The relevant question is whether
the paths contain enough variation for errors to become visible.

Nominally separate studies using the same contaminated dataset may add little.
Groups using different instruments, populations, models, and incentives may
provide stronger convergence.

## Dissent has more than one meaning

A minority view may preserve an ignored possibility. It may also persist
because its advocates are insulated from correction. Mere unpopularity proves
neither courage nor error.

Assess dissent with the same discipline used for consensus: domain,
method, evidence, explanatory performance, prediction, and response to
counterevidence. Romanticizing the dissenter is another form of counting
heads.

## Power can shape agreement

Careers, funding, censorship, publication filters, and social punishment can
reduce visible disagreement. Excluded communities may possess access or
experience the dominant institution lacks.

Power therefore belongs in the audit. But invoking power is not a universal
solvent. Showing that an institution has interests does not show that its
claim is false. One must connect the pressure to a specific distortion in
method, evidence, alternatives, or reporting.

## Consensus can be local and graded

A field may strongly agree that an effect exists while disagreeing about its
magnitude or mechanism. Public summaries often compress this structure into
“experts agree” or “experts are divided.”

Recover the layers:

- what is established;
- what is probable;
- what remains disputed;
- which disagreement affects action;
- which is largely theoretical.

## Consensus has a production history

Agreement at the end of inquiry can arise from repeated correction. Agreement
at the beginning may arise from common training, shared assumptions, or the
simple fact that everyone copied the same map. The visible result is identical:
many people say the same thing. Its epistemic meaning is not.

Reconstruct the production history. Did investigators begin apart and
converge? Were failed approaches preserved? Did methods with different
weaknesses reach compatible results? Could a dissenter state the dominant
case accurately and still work?

A mature consensus is not a choir trained to hold one note. It is what remains
after instruments capable of disagreeing have been allowed to play.

## Counting heads is attractive because it is cheap

The defender of authority counts the majority. The romantic dissenter counts
himself and declares the smaller number proof of courage. Both evade the same
labor: examining how access, method, and correction produced the positions.

Numbers matter. They matter after qualification, independence, and claim
scope have been reconstructed. Before that, a head count is sociology
pretending to have completed epistemology.

## A consensus audit

1. Who counts as a relevant investigator?
2. What precise proposition attracts agreement?
3. How independent are the methods and data?
4. Which correction and publication mechanisms operate?
5. What pressures could suppress visible dissent?
6. How has the community responded to failed predictions?
7. Is the agreement about existence, magnitude, mechanism, or action?

Consensus affects how strongly a finite knower should believe. Yet belief is
too often treated as a switch: on or off, loyal or hostile. The next chapter
replaces the switch with degrees.
