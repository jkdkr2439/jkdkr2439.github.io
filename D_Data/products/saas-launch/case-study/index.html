Clearline SaaS Launch

Marketing ? Ethical Conversion

Overview

A single-page landing for a fictional team communication tool. Proves ethical conversion: transparent pricing, attributed social proof, informed CTA, no dark patterns, dark mode, and progressive enhancement.

Behavioral model

Primary actor: visitor evaluating product value under no time pressure. Trust: pricing visible before commitment, no hidden fees, CTA states what happens next. Agency: no exit-intent popup, no scroll-jacking, no countdown.

Design decisions

Lenses: WCAG (accessibility), Tufte (truthful comparison), Norman (affordance). Fractal 2-pass: pass 1 generic ? pass 2 activated comparison mechanism with progressive disclosure. Section map: hero ? problem ? solution ? proof ? pricing ? CTA ? footer.
